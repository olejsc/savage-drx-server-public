# Savage XR / Battle for Newerth - Beast Tech Tree

## Faction overview

The Beast Horde uses leap mobility, stronger close combat, magic/nature tech, relics, spires, and Gateway-based map pressure. Beasts are harder to master than Humans, but skilled Beast players can dominate melee by attacking angles and conserving stamina.

The Lair is the Beast main base and primary spawn/resupply point. Its destruction ends the match for Beasts.

## Strategic interpretation

| Branch | Strengths | Weaknesses / risks |
|---|---|---|
| Fire | Sacrifice, Blaze, Fireball, Flame Spire, Fire Shield; strong anti-structure and close pressure. | Often requires close delivery; can be countered by detection, mines, Storm Shield-like timing, and ranged focus. |
| Strata | Tempest, Lightning, Storm Shield, Mist Shroud, Wind Spire, Replenish; strong open-map ranged pressure and survivability. | Mana management matters; Wind Spire itself is vulnerable to siege. |
| Entropy | Gateway, Healing Spire, Rupture/Surge, Sixth Sense; strong strategic utility. | Weapons are often considered weaker/situational by the guide. |
| Sanctuary | Shaman heal/revive/shield support. | Red Stone investment and low Shaman HP can reduce offensive mass if overused. |
| Charm Shrine | Summoner and Behemoth siege. | Summoner low HP; Behemoth slow and conspicuous; both need team support. |
