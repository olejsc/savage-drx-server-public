## Beast strategic notes by asset

### Units

- **Scavenger:** Starter labor/combat unit; Leap makes it more mobile than the Human Nomad.
- **Stalker:** Medium Beast unit; capable but often bypassed when the team rushes Predator.
- **Predator:** Signature close-combat unit. Rabid and Carnivorous multiply its staying power.
- **Shaman:** High-value support; Shield Ray is especially important for buildings, siege escorts, and Behemoths.
- **Summoner:** Long-range siege with unlimited shots, but low HP. It must use cover and team protection.
- **Behemoth:** Huge close-range siege unit. It draws fire and needs diversion, Replenish, or Shield Ray support.

### Weapons and upgrades

- **Rabid** is described as the melee champion's default once mastered.
- **Tempest** is one of the most efficient ranged weapons in the guide and can pin Human teams at range.
- **Fireball** is a strong tower/building pressure option from distance.
- **Chaos Bolt**, **Surge**, and **Rupture** are more situational in the guide's view, with Gateway and Healing Spire being the main reasons to value Entropy.

### Relics

- **Sacrifice** is a major Beast anti-structure weapon, especially with Frenzy, Mist Shroud, Storm Shield, or Fire Shield.
- **Storm Shield** completely negates area damage for its duration, making it powerful against mines, chemical weapons, and catapult damage.
- **Mist Shroud** is strongest before Human Sensors are established.
- **Mana Crystal** is important for sustained ranged play, especially Tempest/Lightning users.

### Structures

- **Healing Spire** is the Beast equivalent of a strategic defensive centerpiece; it should be protected.
- **Gateway** from Entropy creates one of the most dangerous strategic threats in the game.
- **Charm Shrine** enables siege and is required for Lair Level 3.
- **Strata Shrine** is valuable because it unlocks Tempest, Storm Shield, Mist Shroud, Wind Spire, and Replenish.
