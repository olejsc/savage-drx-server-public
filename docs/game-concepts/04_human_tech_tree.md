# Savage XR / Battle for Newerth - Human Tech Tree

## Faction overview

The Legion of Man uses industrial structures, mechanical weapons, tower upgrades, portable equipment, and defensive blocking. Humans are easier to learn than Beasts and are generally favored in ranged combat.

## Main base progression

| Stronghold level | Requires | Stone cost | Hit points | Research time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Level 1 | Nothing | None | 25,000 | None |
| Level 2 | Arsenal | 750 Stone | 38,000 | 80 s |
| Level 3 | Siege Workshop + Research Center | 1500 Stone | 50,000 | 120 s |

The Stronghold is the Human main base and primary spawn/resupply point. Its destruction ends the match for Humans.

## Strategic interpretation

| Branch | Strengths | Weaknesses / risks |
|---|---|---|
| Magnetic | Long-range weapons, Coil Rifle, Sensors, Shield Tower, strong defensive value. | Repeater is inaccurate at long range; early branch may be less explosive than Electric/Chemical. |
| Electric | Flux anti-sac defense, Pulse Cannon, Relocaters, 3x damage buff. | Shock Tower is described as expensive and often underwhelming. |
| Chemical | Best anti-structure tools: Demo Pack, Mortar, Launcher, mines, chemical tower. | Short range, low ammo, and lower accuracy can leave team vulnerable on open maps. |
| Monastery | Saves gold through healing/revival and supports siege pushes. | Red Stone investment may reduce offensive field presence on small public teams. |
| Siege Workshop | Ballista/Catapult can end games quickly. | Expensive units are easily wasted without escorts and competent users. |
